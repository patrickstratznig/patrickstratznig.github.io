1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Postleitzahlen Österreich handles information when you use the app. The app provides Austrian postal code and location lookup functionality. The main lookup functionality works offline using data bundled with the app.

2. Data processed by the app

The app allows users to manually search for postal codes, places, and related location information. Manual searches are processed locally on the device and are not sent to our own servers.

The app may optionally access your device location if you tap the location button and grant Android location permission. Location access is used only to determine your approximate or current position in order to show nearby postal code or location information.

3. Location data

Location access is optional. You can use the manual search features of the app without granting location permission.

The app does not request background location access. Location permission is requested only after you actively use a location-based feature in the app.

The app does not store your location history on our servers and does not sell your location data. Location information is used on the device for the requested lookup functionality.

4. Offline data

The app includes postal code and location reference data bundled with the app. This data is used locally on your device. The app does not require an account and does not require an internet connection for the core postal code lookup functionality.

5. Advertising

The app may display small banner advertisements. Ads may be provided by third-party advertising services, such as Google AdMob.

Third-party advertising providers may process data such as device information, advertising identifiers, approximate location, interaction data, diagnostics, and other information according to their own privacy policies and consent settings. This processing is handled by the advertising provider, not by our own servers.

For more information about how Google uses data in advertising services, please visit: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

6. Data sharing

We do not sell user data. We do not operate our own backend server for storing postal code searches or location history.

Data may be processed by third-party services used in the app, especially advertising services, as described above. These third parties process data according to their own privacy policies and legal responsibilities.

7. Data retention and deletion

The app does not create user accounts and does not store personal account data on our servers. The app does not store your location history on our servers.

You can delete locally stored app data at any time through your Android system settings, or by uninstalling the app.

8. Permissions

The app may request location permission if you use the location feature. This permission is used only to provide location-based lookup functionality. The app does not request background location permission.

9. Children

The app is not specifically directed at children. If the app is later made available to children or families, this Privacy Policy and the app's data handling practices may need to be updated accordingly.

10. Security

The app is designed to minimize data processing by performing the core lookup functionality locally on the device. Any data handled by third-party services, such as advertising providers, is subject to those providers' security practices.

11. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the app changes, for example if new features, permissions, analytics, advertising providers, or online services are added. The updated version will be published on this page.
